How it sits in the room

An L-shape. The cooking wall and the sink wall meet in a corner. The sink run's 230 cm is right-aligned to the far end, so it finishes flush under the wall units at the side wall, and a corner unit fills what is left over next to the corner. Panel 20 closes the wall-unit run against the side wall.

The side wall crosses two carcasses — the last wall unit, behind panel 20, and the last base cabinet, behind panel 10. Both are already installed and already cut to that angle, so the angle is an existing condition, not new work.

The grips are drawn to the U12 facts sheet. Each grip is a milled J-pull across the full width of the front: 25 mm tall on the face, 16 mm deep, with an undercut that reaches 30 mm in from the edge. The front is 22 mm thick with sharp, 1 mm-radius sides. Base fronts carry it on top and wall doors at the base, panel 20 included. Panel 6 is the fixed sink front and has none. There is no handle and no visible hinge (Faktum UK, U12).
