Plinth is 4.1 m over both base runs at 145 mm high. Faktum UK cuts it by the millimetre — 2 × 1900 mm plus 1 × 300 mm at their £39.00 minimum. Kitchen Door Workshop sells the matching Cambio plinth in 2540 mm lengths, so two cover the run. Faktum UK prices link straight to the product page and were read from those pages on 30 August 2026. Every size is a listed catalogue item and shows in stock. Kitchen Door Workshop has no per-size list — the figures come from the Handleless Super Matt White pricing tool at those exact millimetre sizes. Their £44.78 repeats on three small fronts, which reads as a minimum charge.

Still open

  1. Panel 20 is cut on site. It is ordered as a tenth 30 × 92 door blank and trimmed to 25 wide, which keeps the grip line running to the end of the run. A made-to-measure flat side panel would be cheaper but would break that line.
  2. Left-to-right order of both base runs — still read off photos, not measured.
  3. Which white — Faktum UK does not publish a sheen for its whites. Get the free swatch kit from both before committing.
  4. Plinth height — priced at 145 mm, the usual FAKTUM leg height. Confirm on site.
  5. Faktum UK delivery cost and lead time — neither is published anywhere on their site. Ask before ordering. Kitchen Door Workshop is free over £500 and quotes a delivery date at checkout.
  6. Noremax is quote-only with a £215 minimum and needs a human to request a price. On profile and finish it is the closest match to the brief after these two.
