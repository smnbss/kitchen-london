Still open

  1. Panel 20 is cut on site. It is ordered as a tenth 30 × 92 door blank and trimmed to 25 wide, which keeps the grip line running to the end of the run. A made-to-measure flat side panel would be cheaper but would break that line.
  2. Left-to-right order of both base runs — still read off photos, not measured.
  3. Which white — Faktum UK does not publish a sheen for its whites. Get the free swatch kit from both before committing.
  4. The wall is at 45°, and panel 20's own measurements prove it. Panel 20 is 25.0 cm on its front face and 23.5 on its back — a 1.5 cm taper across a 16 mm front. An exact 45° wall predicts a taper equal to the panel thickness, so 25 − 1.6 = 23.4 cm against 23.5 measured. That is inside measurement error. The consequence is that a thicker replacement tapers more, because the taper always equals the thickness at 45°:
    PanelThickness FrontBack
    Existing front16 mm25.0 cm23.4 cm
    Kitchen Door Workshop18 mm25.0 cm23.2 cm
    Faktum UK U1222 mm25.0 cm22.8 cm
    Give the supplier the 45° angle, not a pair of widths — the widths change with the panel they make.
  5. Panel 20's angled edge — the finish, not the fit. The bevel crosses the panel's own thickness, not the cabinet depth, so the visible front stays 24 cm and the back edge comes in by roughly the panel thickness. A 30 × 92 blank has ample spare. Both carcasses are already installed and cut, so nothing structural is in question. What remains: a sawn MDF edge carries no lacquer, so ask whether the supplier will cut and finish that edge rather than assuming it happens on site.
  6. ⚠️ The corner unit has no front in this schedule. Right-aligning the sink run leaves about 65 cm at the corner. If that unit carries a door of its own it is a 21st panel nobody has priced, and it would need the same profile and colour as the rest. Confirm whether it takes a front, is a blind corner served by the neighbouring 80, or already has a front you are keeping.
  7. Which plinth wall is which. The 193 cm length is recorded against the cooking wall, whose cabinet run is 180 cm. The 141 on the sink wall is close to the corner unit plus the 80 unit (145). Both gaps are plausible, but check which length belongs to which wall before cutting.
  8. Faktum UK delivery cost and lead time — neither is published anywhere on their site. Ask before ordering. Kitchen Door Workshop is free over £500 and quotes a delivery date at checkout.
  9. Noremax is quote-only with a £215 minimum and needs a human to request a price. On profile and finish it is the closest match to the brief after these two.

How it sits in the room

An L-shape. The cooking wall and the sink wall meet in a corner. The sink run's 230 cm is right-aligned to the far end, so it finishes flush under the wall units at the side wall, and a corner unit fills what is left over next to the corner. Panel 20 closes the wall-unit run against the side wall.

The corner unit is mostly hidden behind the cooking run in this view, which is what it looks like in the room too. The side wall crosses two carcasses — the last wall unit, behind panel 20, and the last base cabinet, behind panel 10. Both are already installed and already cut to that angle, so the angle is an existing condition, not new work.
